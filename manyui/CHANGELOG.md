## 0.1.1

- Export `MBrandColors` with the shared ManyMath light and dark color constants.
- Provide optional bundled brand fonts through the companion `manyui_assets`
  package; system fonts remain the default.

## 0.1.0-dev

- Initial scaffold. No widgets yet.
- Add layered workbench light and dark theme presets with product-oriented
  typography.
- Add `MIconButton` and expand the accessible icon catalog for common app and
  editor actions.
- Keep tooltips inside the overlay viewport by automatically flipping their
  preferred vertical placement and shifting them away from horizontal edges.
- Flip and clamp popovers to the available viewport as anchors move.
- Improve heading, label, list, tree, tab, and text-field semantics and keyboard
  behavior, including autofill hints and accessible secret-field actions.
- Defer initial web focus traversal until layout is available.
